# SOP design contract

- Make the procedure a vertical trace with numbered steps and a strong state
  badge; evidence is nested under the step it supports.
- Keep recommendation and typed outputs in a stable side rail on desktop.
- Put confirmation-required proposals in a full-width safety boundary section.
- Distinguish succeeded, skipped, failed and awaiting-confirmation states by
  label, color and text.
- On mobile, collapse the side rail below the procedure without hiding safety
  copy or evidence.
